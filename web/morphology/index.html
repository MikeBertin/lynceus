What you're seeing. Real JWST/NIRCam cutouts of CEERS galaxies (F200W+F356W+F444W), pulled live from the DAWN JWST Archive cutout service. The three classes — disk (late-type), spheroid (early-type) and compact — are defined from single-Sérsic profile fits (van der Wel et al. 2025): n < 1.2, n > 2.5, and effective radius below 0.09″. The ViT never sees those numbers — it learns morphology straight from the pixels. The compact class is where the mysterious Little Red Dots hide; surfacing them is the next milestone's anomaly hunt.

Lynceus — the keenest-sighted of the Argonauts. Companion to Chiron, Empedocles and Plutus. Model: ViT-Small (timm, ImageNet-pretrained) fine-tuned + INT8-quantised, via onnxruntime-web.
