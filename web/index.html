Why "Lynceus"

Lynceus sailed with the Argonauts and had the sharpest sight of any mortal — he could pick out a ship on the horizon, see through earth and water, and find what others missed. That is the whole task here: the faintest galaxies in James Webb's deepest images sit at the edge of the noise, and the most interesting objects are the ones that don't look like anything else. Lynceus learns to see them.

Lynceus · a serious research core + interactive demos. Built with PyTorch + timm, served as static ONNX.
